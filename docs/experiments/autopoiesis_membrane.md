# Autopoiesis / Membrane Formation

**Category:** Artificial Life  
**ID:** `autopoiesis_membrane`  
**Experimental:** No

---

## Purpose

Explore boundary formation, inside/outside differentiation and self-maintaining structures.
Inspired by autopoiesis and protocell research — this is a conceptual ALife preset.

---

## Inspiration

Maturana & Varela (1972) defined autopoiesis as a system that continuously reproduces its
own boundary conditions. Luisi & Szostak's protocell research explores how simple chemistry
can form self-enclosing membranes.

This preset uses strong coherence coupling and high memory retention to explore whether
the abstract field self-organises into persistent bounded regions — "inside" vs "outside"
differentiation without explicit boundary chemistry.

No biochemistry, lipid bilayers or metabolism are modelled.

---

## How to Run

**Dashboard:** Select *Artificial Life → Autopoiesis / Membrane Formation* in the sidebar, click **▶ Apply Preset & Reset**.

**CLI:**
```bash
python examples/run_preset.py --preset autopoiesis_membrane --steps 600
```

## Key Parameters

| Parameter | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `coupling_gain` | 0.15 | Strong lateral coupling = boundary formation |
| `coupling_sync_rate` | 0.07 | High synchronisation = coherent interior |
| `memory_decay` | 0.985 | Slow decay = boundaries persist |
| `reaction_strength` | 0.12 | Local transformation = self-maintenance proxy |

---

## Expected Patterns

- Localised coherent regions with sharper boundaries
- Inside/outside coherence differentiation
- Temporary self-maintaining clusters
- Boundary-like coupling structures at region edges

---

## Suggested Observations

1. Watch the **coherence field** — bounded regions should emerge with higher interior coherence.
2. Compare **coupling** and **coherence** fields — boundaries appear as coupling ridges.
3. Check the **Proto-Leben** score in the Lernen tab — self-maintaining structures should score higher.
4. Run for 500+ ticks and observe whether boundaries persist or dissolve.
5. Vary `coupling_gain` (0.10 vs 0.20) — too low → no boundaries; too high → locked grids.

---

## Suggested Metrics

- Boundary sharpness (coherence field gradient magnitude)
- Cluster persistence over time
- Inside/outside coherence contrast
- Proto-life score from consciousness module

---

## Limitations

- No explicit membrane chemistry or lipid bilayer analogue
- No metabolism — energy is not consumed, transformed or regenerated by the structure
- No reproduction — structures do not self-replicate
- "Inside/outside" is an interpretive reading, not a measured quantity yet
