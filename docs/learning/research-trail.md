# Research Trail

Curated resources for exploring complex systems science, artificial life,
information theory, morphogenesis and emergent simulation.

All resources are verifiable references. URLs may change over time.

---

## Books

### Beginner / Intermediate

**Complexity: A Guided Tour** — Melanie Mitchell (2009)
The most accessible introduction to complexity science, emergence and computation.
Covers cellular automata, genetic algorithms, network science and information theory.

**Artificial Life: A Report from the Frontier** — Steven Levy (1992)
Narrative history of the ALife field — people, ideas, and early digital organisms.
Excellent starting point for the cultural and intellectual context.

**The Nature of Code** — Daniel Shiffman (2024)
Creative coding guide covering cellular automata, flocking, neural nets and more.
Interactive online version: https://natureofcode.com/
Cellular automata chapter: https://natureofcode.com/cellular-automata/

**Sync: The Emerging Science of Spontaneous Order** — Steven H. Strogatz (2003)
Self-organisation, synchronisation and coupled oscillators.
Accessible and beautifully written.

### Intermediate / Advanced

**The Computational Beauty of Nature** — Gary William Flake (1998)
Covers chaos, fractals, complex systems and adaptation with clear mathematics.

**A New Kind of Science** — Stephen Wolfram (2002)
Wolfram's exploration of cellular automata and computational equivalence.
Free online: https://www.wolframscience.com/nks/

## Papers

**The Chemical Basis of Morphogenesis** — Alan M. Turing (1952)
DOI: 10.1098/rstb.1952.0012
Introduces reaction-diffusion systems as a model of pattern formation.
Surprisingly readable for a 1952 paper.

**Flocks, Herds and Schools: A Distributed Behavioral Model** — Craig W. Reynolds (1987)
DOI: 10.1145/37401.37406
Three simple rules that produce lifelike flocking behaviour (Boids).

**Lenia: Biology of Artificial Life** — Bert Wang-Chak Chan (2019)
arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.05433 | DOI: 10.25088/ComplexSystems.28.3.251
Continuous generalisation of Conway's Game of Life with rich morphological dynamics.

**Rules for Biologically Inspired Adaptive Network Design** — Tero et al. (2010)
DOI: 10.1126/science.1177894
Physarum polycephalum (slime mould) solves transport network optimisation problems.
Foundation for mycelium-inspired models.

**Consciousness as Integrated Information** — Giulio Tononi (2008)
DOI: 10.1007/s00422-008-0236-4
Foundational paper on IIT and the Phi measure. (Research level)

**The informational architecture of the cell** — Sara Imari Walker & Paul C. W. Davies (2013)
DOI: 10.1098/rsif.2012.0869
Top-down causation and information in living systems. (Research level)

## Projects

**Lenia (interactive)** — Bert Wang-Chak Chan
https://chakazul.github.io/lenia.html
Interactive web demo of continuous cellular automata. Many patterns available.

**Avida-ED** — Digital Evolution Platform
https://avida-ed.github.io/
Self-replicating programs that evolve. Educational platform for digital evolution.

**OpenWorm**
https://openworm.org/
Full simulation of C. elegans: 302 neurons, complete connectome, muscle system.

**International Society for Artificial Life (ISAL)**
https://alife.org/
Research community, annual conference (ALIFE), proceedings archive.
